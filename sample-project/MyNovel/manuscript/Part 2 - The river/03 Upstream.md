# Upstream

The Sel ran against them all morning.

Arno kept the boat close to the left bank, where the current was weaker, and watched Elena count the bridges. She did it under her breath, the way a child counts stairs in the dark.

It was a trick Mara had taught her, the winter the river froze. Counting kept the fear small, Mara said, the way a lid keeps a pot quiet. He did not ask who Mara was.

***

By noon she had stopped counting. Kell took that as a bad sign.
