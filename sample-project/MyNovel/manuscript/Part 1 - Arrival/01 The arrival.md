# The arrival

Elena reached the Harbor Inn at dusk, *soaked to the bone*.

The ferryman was already waiting at the bar. He had a glass in front of him that he had not touched, and he watched the door the way other men watch a fire.

“You’re late,” said Arno Kell.

“The road from Varn was under water.” Elena dropped her bag by the stool. “Half of it, anyway. The other half was mud.”

***

Morning came grey over the river.

From the window of her room Elena could see the whole of the Sel, wide and brown and quick, and the long black hull of Kell’s boat tied up below the Harbor Inn. **Three days upstream**, he had said. She had not asked what waited at the end of them.
