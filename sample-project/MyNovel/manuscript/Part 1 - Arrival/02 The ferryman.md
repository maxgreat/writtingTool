# The ferryman

The ferryman did not haggle. He wrote a number on the back of a receipt and slid it across the table, and when Elena turned it over there was no number at all, only a name she had not said aloud in eleven years.

> Every crossing has a price. Mine is a story, told true.

Elena read the line twice. It was painted on the inside of the boat’s door, in letters gone pale with river damp.

“I don’t tell stories,” she said.

Kell smiled for the first time. “Everyone says that at the Harbor Inn. By Varn they’ve changed their minds.”
