Privacy policy

Writing Tool collects no personal data. What you write is kept on your device, and the app sends nothing to the developer or to anyone else.

Effective 23 November 2026. This policy covers the Writing Tool app on Android, Windows, macOS and Linux.

What the app collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking.

Where your writing is kept

Your projects are folders of Markdown files on your device. New projects, and the sample project the app adds on first launch, are kept in the app’s own data folder. On Windows, macOS and Linux you can also open a project folder you choose, and the app reads and saves it where it is.

The app’s settings, and on desktop the list of folders you opened recently, are kept on your device too. Nothing you write is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Network access

The app makes no network requests. Its source code is public, so anyone can check this.

On Android the app has the Internet permission, because Tauri, the framework it is built with, includes it. Writing Tool doesn’t use it to send anything.

Uninstalling and backups on Android

Uninstalling the app on Android deletes its data folder and the projects in it. The app can’t copy projects out of its own storage yet, so keep that in mind before uninstalling it.

If backup to your Google account is turned on in Android’s settings, Android may include the app’s data folder in that backup and restore it when you install the app again. That copy is made by Android and kept in your Google account. The developer has no access to it.

Changes to this policy

Future versions may add optional cloud sync; Dropbox is planned. If a version adds anything that sends data off your device, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and it will say what is sent and to whom.

The date at the top is when this version took effect. Earlier versions are in this page’s history.

Children

Writing Tool isn’t directed at children under 13. It collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the app, open an issue at github.com/maxgreat/writtingTool/issues. Issues are public, so don’t include anything private.
