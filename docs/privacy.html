Privacy policy

Writing Tool collects no personal data. What you write is kept on your device, and the app sends nothing to the developer. It sends text to anyone else only if you turn on the LanguageTool grammar check, and only to the server you choose.

Effective 25 November 2026. This policy covers the Writing Tool app on Android, Windows, macOS and Linux.

What the app collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking.

Where your writing is kept

Your projects are folders of Markdown files on your device. New projects, and the sample project the app adds on first launch, are kept in the app’s own data folder. On Windows, macOS and Linux you can also open a project folder you choose, and the app reads and saves it where it is. When you export a book, the file is saved where you choose in the save dialog.

The app’s settings, and on desktop the list of folders you opened recently, are kept on your device too, including the LanguageTool user name and API key if you enter them. Nothing you write is sent to the developer.

Network access

The app makes no network requests unless you turn on the LanguageTool grammar check in Settings. It is off unless you choose a server. Spelling, and grammar in English, are checked on your device. Its source code is public, so anyone can check this.

When you choose a LanguageTool server, the paragraphs of the chapter you are writing are sent to it to be checked, a moment after you stop typing, and the problems it finds come back. While the app is open, a paragraph that hasn’t changed isn’t sent again, unless the server couldn’t answer; the next time the app opens, or after you change the project’s language or the server, the paragraphs are checked again. The server is the one you choose: LanguageTool’s free server at languagetool.org or your LanguageTool Premium account, whose privacy policy covers what they receive, or a server of your own. With a Premium account your user name and API key are sent with each check. Turning the grammar server off in Settings stops this at once.

On Android the app has the Internet permission. Writing Tool uses it only for the LanguageTool grammar check, when you turn it on.

Uninstalling and backups on Android

Uninstalling the app on Android deletes its data folder and the projects in it. Export can save a copy of a book’s chapters (as PDF, EPUB, Word or Markdown) where you choose, but the project itself, with its character and place sheets and notes, can’t be copied out of the app’s storage yet, so keep that in mind before uninstalling it.

If backup to your Google account is turned on in Android’s settings, Android may include the app’s data folder in that backup and restore it when you install the app again. That copy is made by Android and kept in your Google account. The developer has no access to it.

Changes to this policy

Future versions may add optional cloud sync; Dropbox is planned. If a version adds anything that sends data off your device, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and it will say what is sent and to whom.

The date at the top is when this version took effect. Earlier versions are in this page’s history.

Children

Writing Tool isn’t directed at children under 13. It collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the app, open an issue at github.com/maxgreat/writtingTool/issues. Issues are public, so don’t include anything private.
